# Community conduct

Keep discussion constructive, specific and welcoming. Critique technical claims with evidence; respect differences in experience, language and background. Do not harass, discriminate, expose private information or use issues for promotion unrelated to the catalogue.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict participation. Report a conduct problem through the repository owner's contact links; use private vulnerability reporting only for security issues. Corrections and respectful disagreement are welcome.

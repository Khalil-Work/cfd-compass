## Read the labels

**Established / research / emerging** are editorial context labels. **Archived** and last-push dates are GitHub metadata. **NOASSERTION** means automated licence identification is inconclusive. **Source-available restrictions and maintenance notices appear in the notes.** Inclusion and passing catalogue CI do not establish scientific accuracy or successful installation. Stars are dated discovery metadata in the searchable directory, not a solver ranking. [Methodology →](docs/METHODOLOGY.md)

## 🌊 General-purpose solvers & frameworks

Finite-volume and finite-element starting points for engineering flow simulation.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[ADflow](https://github.com/mdolab/adflow)**<br>Gradient-based aerodynamic shape optimization | Compressible Euler, laminar Navier-Stokes and RANS solver on structured multiblock and overset meshes, with a discrete adjoint and derivative-verification facilities for aerodynamic design optimization.<br>[Docs](https://mdolab-adflow.readthedocs-hosted.com/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Targets structured and overset design workflows; a mesh generator and the surrounding MDO dependencies may be required for complete optimization studies. |
| **[code_saturne](https://github.com/code-saturne/code_saturne)**<br>Industrial thermal-fluid simulations | EDF's general-purpose CFD system for industrial flow and transport studies, providing finite-volume numerics, turbulence and heat-transfer models, parallel execution, and associated case setup tooling.<br>[Docs](https://code-saturne.org/en) · `GPL-2.0` | Established · The project describes this GitHub repository as its public mirror; follow official release and contribution instructions rather than treating every branch as a release. |
| **[COOLFluiD](https://github.com/andrealani/COOLFluiD)**<br>CFD and plasma solver development | Object-oriented high-performance platform for computational fluid dynamics, plasma and multiphysics simulations, exposing a modular research environment for developing and combining numerical methods and physical models.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/andrealani/COOLFluiD/wiki) · `LGPL-3.0` | Research · Check the relevant plugin documentation and licensing details; this is a developer-oriented platform with a substantial build configuration. |
| **[Elmer FEM](https://github.com/ElmerCSC/elmerfem)**<br>Coupled finite-element thermal-fluid problems | Multiphysics finite-element package combining fluid mechanics, heat transfer and other field solvers, with mesh and graphical tools plus parallel infrastructure for coupled scientific simulations.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/ElmerCSC/elmerfem#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Select the solver module, discretization and coupling strategy explicitly; breadth of available physics does not imply every coupling is turnkey. |
| **[FiPy](https://github.com/usnistgov/fipy)**<br>Transport-equation and phase-field prototypes | Python finite-volume PDE framework from NIST for assembling coupled convection, diffusion, transient and source terms, particularly useful for transport, phase-field and materials-model prototyping and research.<br>[Docs](https://pages.nist.gov/fipy/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · A PDE-building framework rather than a complete industrial Navier-Stokes workflow; users must formulate and verify their coupled equations. |
| **[Fluidity](https://github.com/FluidityProject/fluidity)**<br>Anisotropic adaptive flow simulations | Unstructured finite-element and control-volume CFD code with anisotropic mesh adaptivity, parallel execution and Python interfaces, used for multiphase, geophysical and general fluid-dynamics simulations.<br>[Docs](https://fluidityproject.github.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · The dependency and platform setup is substantial; use installation instructions matching the chosen branch and environment. |
| **[Kratos Multiphysics](https://github.com/KratosMultiphysics/Kratos)**<br>Coupled fluid-structure engineering applications | Modular C++ multiphysics framework with Python interfaces and dedicated fluid-dynamics, fluid-structure interaction and particle applications, supporting coupled engineering workflows and parallel simulation development.<br>[Docs](https://kratosmultiphysics.github.io/Kratos/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Capabilities belong to specific applications; the core framework alone is not a configured CFD solver. |
| **[Lethe](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe)**<br>Adaptive CFD and CFD-DEM research | Finite-element CFD and discrete-element project using deal.II, with high-order continuous Galerkin formulations, adaptive meshes and coupled fluid-particle solvers for single-phase and multiphase engineering research.<br>[Docs](https://chaos-polymtl.github.io/lethe/index.html) · `Apache-2.0` | Research · Requires a compatible deal.II dependency stack; choose the relevant solver and reproduce its documented benchmark before extending a model. |
| **[MOOSE Navier-Stokes](https://github.com/idaholab/moose)**<br>Custom coupled flow applications | Navier-Stokes module within the MOOSE multiphysics framework, offering finite-element and finite-volume formulations for pressure-based, compressible and porous-flow applications with shared nonlinear-solver infrastructure and coupling mechanisms.<br>[Docs](https://mooseframework.inl.gov/modules/navier_stokes/) · `LGPL-2.1` | Established · Read the module's solver-capability matrix: turbulence, two-phase and compressibility support varies between formulations and is actively evolving. |
| **[OpenFOAM Foundation](https://github.com/OpenFOAM/OpenFOAM-dev)**<br>Extensible engineering CFD workflows | Finite-volume CFD platform with an extensive solver, physical-model and meshing ecosystem, covering incompressible and compressible flows, turbulence, heat transfer, reacting systems and multiphase problems.<br>[Docs](https://doc.cfd.direct/openfoam/user-guide/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Foundation development branch; use a release for a fixed baseline. OpenCFD's separately maintained OpenFOAM distribution has a different upstream and release scheme. |
| **[SU2](https://github.com/su2code/SU2)**<br>Aerodynamic analysis and adjoint optimization | Multiphysics analysis and design suite centered on CFD and aerodynamic optimization, combining flow solvers, adjoint methods and Python-driven workflows for PDE-constrained engineering design studies.<br>[Docs](https://su2code.github.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Check the configuration and physical-model support for the specific SU2 release; example coverage does not establish accuracy for a new design. |

## 🔷 High-order & spectral methods

Discontinuous Galerkin, spectral-element and high-order discretizations.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[ExaDG](https://github.com/exadg/exadg)**<br>Matrix-free high-order incompressible CFD | Matrix-free high-order discontinuous Galerkin project whose core incompressible Navier-Stokes solver targets LES and DNS, with additional flow-transport, moving-mesh, fluid-structure and compressible-flow capabilities for research applications.<br>[Docs](https://exadg.github.io/exadg/) · `GPL-3.0` | Research · Best aligned with quadrilateral/hexahedral high-order discretizations; installation requires a compatible finite-element and linear-algebra dependency stack. |
| **[FLEXI](https://github.com/flexi-framework/flexi)**<br>High-order compressible-flow simulations | Discontinuous Galerkin spectral-element framework for hyperbolic-parabolic conservation laws, emphasizing compressible CFD on unstructured hexahedral meshes with parallel execution and a supporting preprocessing and postprocessing suite.<br>[Docs](https://www.flexi-project.org/) · `GPL-3.0` | Research · Uses a specific high-order mesh and preprocessing workflow; check the required HOPR and HDF5 versions for the chosen release. |
| **[HORSES3D](https://github.com/horses-framework/horses3d)**<br>Adaptive high-order multiphysics flow studies | Fortran DG spectral-element multiphysics solver for compressible and incompressible flows, with polynomial adaptivity, turbulence closures and diffuse-interface multiphase formulations on curvilinear hexahedral meshes using hybrid CPU parallelism.<br>[Docs](https://horses-framework.github.io/horses3d/) · `MIT` | Research · The CPU repository and separate GPU project have different supported paths; verify that a desired feature is available in the selected implementation. |
| **[MIRGE-Com](https://github.com/illinois-ceesd/mirgecom)**<br>Research on reacting compressible DG solvers | Python-driven discontinuous Galerkin simulation application for compressible viscous and inviscid reactive mixtures, using unstructured meshes and accelerator-oriented infrastructure for exascale-enabled scramjet and fluid-flow research.<br>[Docs](https://mirgecom.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Research application with a specialized array, mesh and code-generation stack; inspect supported chemistry and transport assumptions before selecting it for a study. |
| **[Nek5000](https://github.com/Nek5000/Nek5000)**<br>High-order incompressible DNS and heat transfer | Spectral-element solver for incompressible and low-Mach flows, scalar transport and conjugate heat transfer, using high-order tensor-product elements and parallel algorithms suited to turbulence research.<br>[Docs](https://nek5000.github.io/NekDoc/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Classic CPU-oriented Nek implementation; mesh and polynomial resolution must be selected for the intended flow and turbulence regime. |
| **[nekRS](https://github.com/Nek5000/nekRS)**<br>GPU-oriented spectral-element CFD | High-order spectral-element CFD solver for incompressible and low-Mach flow, with OCCA-based CPU and GPU backends, scalar transport, turbulence models and conjugate heat-transfer capabilities on HPC systems.<br>[Docs](https://nekrsdoc.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Follow the release notes and backend requirements; the project distinguishes stable master from preview development and does not support every desktop platform. |
| **[PyFR](https://github.com/PyFR/PyFR)**<br>High-order scale-resolving engineering flows | Flux-reconstruction CFD platform for high-order scale-resolving flow simulations on unstructured mixed-element meshes, with code-generated computational kernels and multiple CPU and GPU execution backends for heterogeneous systems.<br>[Docs](https://pyfr.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Backend, mesh-element and equation-system support depend on the selected release; compare cost at equivalent accuracy rather than polynomial order alone. |
| **[Trixi.jl](https://github.com/trixi-framework/Trixi.jl)**<br>Adaptive high-order conservation-law research | Julia framework for high-order conservation-law simulations, combining discontinuous Galerkin and related methods, adaptive tree-based meshes and an extensible equation interface for compressible-flow and numerical-method research.<br>[Docs](https://trixi-framework.org/TrixiDocumentation/stable/) · `MIT` | Research · Check equation, mesh and backend compatibility in the documentation; Julia's first-run compilation cost should be separated from steady-state timings. |
| **[UCNS3D](https://github.com/ucns3d-team/UCNS3D)**<br>High-order compressible CFD on mixed meshes | Unstructured compressible-flow solver offering high-order finite-volume reconstruction and discontinuous Galerkin methods, mixed-element meshes, turbulence models and MPI/OpenMP execution for aerodynamic and multicomponent-flow research in two or three dimensions.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/ucns3d-team/UCNS3D#readme) · `GPL-3.0` | Research · Build bounds can be case-specific, and accelerator offload covers selected paths rather than every solver mode; follow the current build instructions. |

## 🌀 Turbulence, DNS, LES & HPC

Research solvers, adaptive grids and large-scale flow computing.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[AFiD](https://github.com/PhysicsofFluids/AFiD)**<br>Canonical convection and rotating-wall DNS | Parallel finite-difference flow solver for Rayleigh-Benard convection and Taylor-Couette turbulence, built around distributed decompositions and FFT-based algorithms for large canonical direct numerical simulations on HPC systems.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/PhysicsofFluids/AFiD#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Specialized canonical-flow geometry and boundary conditions; check variant-specific documentation and dependencies rather than treating it as a general geometry solver. |
| **[CaNS](https://github.com/CaNS-World/CaNS)**<br>Canonical wall-bounded turbulence DNS | Parallel incompressible Navier-Stokes solver designed for canonical-flow DNS, using a structured finite-difference discretization and efficient pressure solution with CPU and GPU-oriented implementations for turbulence research at scale.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/CaNS-World/CaNS#readme) · `MIT` | Research · Targets canonical structured domains; check grid, boundary-condition and accelerator constraints instead of assuming arbitrary industrial geometry support. |
| **[Dedalus](https://github.com/DedalusProject/dedalus)**<br>Custom spectral fluid-dynamics models | Spectral PDE framework with symbolic equation specification and parallel execution, supporting fluid-dynamics research across Cartesian and curved domains rather than restricting users to one fixed equation set.<br>[Docs](https://dedalus-project.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `GPL-3.0` | Established · Users formulate equations, gauge conditions and boundary constraints; this flexibility is not a substitute for checking mathematical well-posedness and numerical convergence. |
| **[FluidSim](https://github.com/fluiddyn/fluidsim)**<br>Reproducible Python turbulence experiments | Python framework for reproducible fluid-dynamics simulations with extensible solvers, MPI-enabled pseudospectral methods, and integrated output and analysis workflows for turbulence and geophysical-flow research across multiple model equations.<br>[Docs](https://fluidsim.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Official publish-only GitHub mirror; canonical development and contributions are on the project's Heptapod Mercurial repository. |
| **[IAMR](https://github.com/AMReX-Fluids/IAMR)**<br>Variable-density incompressible AMR studies | Parallel adaptive-mesh code for variable-density incompressible Navier-Stokes equations, providing an AMReX-based route to locally refined flow simulations and exploration of block-structured algorithms for research applications.<br>[Docs](https://amrex-fluids.github.io/IAMR/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Variable-density incompressibility is a modeling assumption, not a fully compressible shock-capturing formulation; check the examples and required AMReX revision. |
| **[incflo](https://github.com/AMReX-Fluids/incflo)**<br>Adaptive embedded-boundary incompressible CFD | AMReX-based incompressible Navier-Stokes application for flows in complex geometries, combining block-structured adaptive-grid infrastructure with embedded boundaries and shared hydrodynamic utilities for parallel CPU and GPU research.<br>[Docs](https://amrex-fluids.github.io/incflo/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · Requires compatible AMReX and AMReX-Hydro versions; follow project instructions for supported solvers and boundary conditions rather than assuming general model coverage. |
| **[Oceananigans.jl](https://github.com/CliMA/Oceananigans.jl)**<br>GPU geophysical and ocean turbulence | Julia software for incompressible geophysical fluid dynamics on CPU and GPU hardware, supporting Cartesian and spherical-shell domains, turbulence studies and ocean-oriented models with programmable simulation workflows.<br>[Docs](https://clima.github.io/OceananigansDocumentation/stable/) · `MIT` | Established · Ocean-oriented incompressible and hydrostatic/nonhydrostatic modeling assumptions differ from general compressible industrial CFD; choose the documented model appropriate to the scales. Its documented ocean and Boussinesq formulations do not cover arbitrary compressible engineering flows. |
| **[OpenSBLI](https://github.com/opensbli/opensbli)**<br>Generated compressible DNS solvers | Python code-generation framework translating symbolic compressible-flow equations into parallel high-order finite-difference solvers, with structured curvilinear multiblock meshes and WENO or TENO shock-capturing options for heterogeneous computing architectures.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/opensbli/opensbli#readme) · `GPL-3.0` | Research · Generated solvers depend on a compatible parallel runtime and compiler stack; changing symbolic equations still requires numerical verification. |
| **[Pencil Code](https://github.com/pencil-code/pencil-code)**<br>Compressible turbulence and MHD research | Modular high-order finite-difference code for compressible hydrodynamics with magnetic fields and particles, offering MPI-parallel infrastructure and many sample configurations for turbulence and astrophysical-fluid research on distributed systems.<br>[Docs](https://pencil-code.org/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · The project documents its own source-management and contribution workflow; numerical diffusion and shock treatment must be selected for the intended regime. |
| **[spectralDNS](https://github.com/spectralDNS/spectralDNS)**<br>Python spectral DNS research | Python collection of parallel spectral Navier-Stokes and related solvers, providing an inspectable environment for direct numerical simulation and experiments with spectral discretization and time-integration methods.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/spectralDNS/spectralDNS#readme) · `LGPL-3.0` | Research · Research-oriented solver collection; verify the maintained solver path and current shenfun/MPI dependency compatibility for the intended example. |
| **[WaterLily.jl](https://github.com/WaterLily-jl/WaterLily.jl)**<br>Scripted immersed-body flow experiments | Julia solver for unsteady incompressible viscous flow around dynamic immersed bodies, using Cartesian grids, geometric multigrid pressure solves and backend-agnostic CPU or GPU execution in two or three dimensions.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/WaterLily-jl/WaterLily.jl#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · An immersed Cartesian-grid method rather than a body-fitted mesh solver; quantify boundary and grid-resolution effects for the target geometry. |
| **[Xcompact3d / Incompact3d](https://github.com/xcompact3d/Incompact3d)**<br>Structured-grid incompressible DNS and LES | High-order compact finite-difference framework for turbulent-flow DNS and LES, combining Cartesian-grid efficiency, distributed-memory parallelism and immersed-boundary treatments for research on incompressible flows and complex geometries.<br>[Docs](https://xcompact3d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Established · The named repository is the CPU solver; inspect the separate accelerator developments rather than assuming identical GPU capabilities. |

## ⚛️ Lattice Boltzmann & particle methods

Kinetic, mesoscopic and particle-based fluid formulations.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[DualSPHysics](https://github.com/DualSPHysics/DualSPHysics)**<br>Free-surface waves and impact problems | Smoothed-particle hydrodynamics solver using C++, CUDA and OpenMP, with engineering workflows focused on free surfaces, wave interactions and large-deformation fluid motion around moving bodies and structures.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/DualSPHysics/DualSPHysics#readme) · `LGPL-2.1` | Established · The complete user distribution includes associated tools beyond the source repository; particle resolution, artificial compressibility and boundary treatment require case-specific assessment. Particle resolution, boundary treatment and weak-compressibility settings influence results; GPU builds require a supported CUDA toolchain. |
| **[FluidX3D](https://github.com/ProjectPhysX/FluidX3D)**<br>Interactive GPU lattice-Boltzmann studies | OpenCL lattice-Boltzmann flow simulator with CPU and GPU execution, integrated visualization, free-surface and thermal extensions, and tools for importing triangulated geometry into a Cartesian lattice for interactive studies.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/ProjectPhysX/FluidX3D/blob/master/DOCUMENTATION.md) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Source-available with a non-commercial restriction, explicitly not OSI open source. Check its current license before use; the documented solver has no checkpoint/restart support. |
| **[GPUSPH](https://github.com/GPUSPH/gpusph)**<br>GPU weakly compressible SPH | CUDA implementation of weakly compressible smoothed-particle hydrodynamics, providing GPU-oriented infrastructure for free-surface flow simulations and research involving moving boundaries and fluid interactions with engineering geometries.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/GPUSPH/gpusph#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · CUDA hardware and a compatible compiler stack are required; establish the chosen formulation's resolution and compressibility error for the case. |
| **[HemoCell](https://github.com/UvaCsl/HemoCell)**<br>Resolved blood-cell suspension simulations | Cell-resolved suspension framework coupling Palabos lattice-Boltzmann fluid dynamics to deformable Lagrangian cell membranes through immersed boundaries, with dedicated models and parallel data management for blood-flow research.<br>[Docs](https://hemocell.eu/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · A specialized cellular-flow model, not a clinical prediction tool; inspect cell mechanics, material parameters and case-specific validation requirements. |
| **[Lettuce](https://github.com/lettucecfd/lettuce)**<br>LBM and machine-learning experiments | PyTorch-based lattice-Boltzmann framework for two- and three-dimensional fluid simulations, enabling Python prototyping, GPU execution and experiments connecting kinetic flow solvers with neural networks and automatic differentiation.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/lettucecfd/lettuce#readme) · `MIT` | Research · Check current PyTorch/CUDA compatibility and whether custom kernels support the intended differentiable workflow; default installation targets specific CUDA versions. |
| **[NATriuM](https://github.com/lettucecfd/NATriuM)**<br>Off-lattice kinetic-method research reference | High-order off-lattice Boltzmann research solver based on deal.II, supporting unstructured two- and three-dimensional grids through discontinuous-Galerkin or semi-Lagrangian streaming for weakly and fully compressible viscous flows.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/lettucecfd/NATriuM#readme) · `MIT` | Research · Authors explicitly warn that the code is early-stage and they are unlikely to answer questions; treat it as a research reference with limited support. |
| **[PySPH](https://github.com/pypr/pysph)**<br>Reproducible SPH method development | Python framework for defining particle equations and integrators, generating compiled Cython or OpenCL kernels, and running several SPH formulations with optional shared-memory and distributed parallelism for research simulations.<br>[Docs](https://pysph.readthedocs.io/en/main/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Different bundled SPH formulations make different assumptions; compare conservation, convergence and boundary behavior for the scheme actually used. A method-development framework: selecting an available formulation does not establish suitability for a particular engineering problem. |
| **[Sailfish](https://github.com/sailfish-team/sailfish)**<br>Reference GPU lattice-Boltzmann implementations | GPU lattice-Boltzmann simulation package with a Python-driven setup and CUDA or OpenCL execution, providing examples for exploring kinetic flow models and generated computational kernels on supported accelerators.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/sailfish-team/sailfish#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Legacy dependency and GPU-toolchain assumptions need checking before adoption; its historical examples should not be assumed compatible with a current Python environment. |
| **[SPHinXsys](https://github.com/Xiangyu-Hu/SPHinXsys)**<br>Particle-based coupled fluid-solid models | C++ multiphysics library built around smoothed-particle hydrodynamics, providing unified discretizations and strongly coupled fluid, solid and multibody models together with examples and a Python interface for engineering studies.<br>[Docs](https://www.sphinxsys.org/html/sphinx_index.html) · `Apache-2.0` | Research · Select the specific formulation and tested example family; a common particle framework does not eliminate resolution or coupling-error studies. Research and engineering library with model-specific assumptions; choose examples matching the intended constitutive laws and flow regime. |

## 🔥 Combustion, chemistry & reacting flows

Chemical kinetics, flames, reacting-flow solvers and fire simulation.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[ABLATE](https://github.com/UBCHREST/ablate)**<br>Hybrid-rocket combustion and turbulent entrainment | Develops reacting-flow capabilities around turbulent mixing, fuel entrainment and ablative boundary layers. Offers a research entry point for studying combustion environments associated with hybrid rocket motors.<br>[Docs](https://ablate.dev/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · Research-oriented software; use documented examples and confirm the available physical models before planning a new application. |
| **[Cantera](https://github.com/Cantera/cantera)**<br>Ignition, flame-speed and mechanism studies | Provides thermochemistry, transport properties, reaction mechanisms, reactor networks and one-dimensional flames. A practical foundation for checking chemistry assumptions before embedding them in a reacting-flow solver.<br>[Docs](https://cantera.org/stable/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Not a general three-dimensional CFD solver; mechanism suitability must be assessed for the intended conditions. |
| **[Fire Dynamics Simulator](https://github.com/firemodels/fds)**<br>Fire, smoke and building-flow research | Models low-speed fire-driven flows with large-eddy simulation, emphasizing smoke and heat transport. Its documentation includes separate verification and validation material for assessing intended fire-engineering applications.<br>[Docs](https://pages.nist.gov/fds-smv/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · A low-speed fire model rather than a general shock or detonation solver. Safety-critical conclusions require qualified interpretation and appropriate validation. |
| **[PeleC](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PeleC)**<br>Compressible combustion and shock-flame interaction | Solves compressible reacting flows using block-structured adaptive meshes and embedded boundaries. Connects detailed chemistry to flow calculations where acoustic waves and compressibility are physically important.<br>[Docs](https://pele-suite.github.io/PeleC/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Requires the AMReX, PelePhysics and SUNDIALS stack; mesh, chemistry and temporal resolution need problem-specific assessment. |
| **[PeleLMeX](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PeleLMeX)**<br>Low-Mach turbulent combustion | Combines a low-Mach reacting-flow formulation with adaptive refinement and embedded boundaries. Targets flame-turbulence research while avoiding the acoustic time-scale restriction of a fully compressible formulation.<br>[Docs](https://pele-suite.github.io/PeleLMeX/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · The low-Mach formulation is unsuitable when shocks or strong acoustic coupling govern the physics. Use the documented model assumptions. |
| **[PelePhysics](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PelePhysics)**<br>Chemistry, spray and soot infrastructure | Supplies thermodynamics, transport, chemistry generation and additional spray, soot and radiation modules shared by Pele solvers.

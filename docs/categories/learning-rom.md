## 🧠 Scientific ML, differentiable CFD & ROM

Physics-informed learning, operators, differentiable models and reduced-order methods.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[DAPPER](https://github.com/nansencenter/DAPPER)**<br>Data-assimilation method comparisons | Experimental data-assimilation framework with ensemble and variational methods, diagnostics and model examples. Useful for learning state estimation and testing assimilation ideas on geophysical-fluid prototypes before solver-specific integration.<br>[Docs](https://nansencenter.github.io/DAPPER/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Designed for teaching and fundamental experiments; coupling production CFD requires additional implementation. |
| **[DeepXDE](https://github.com/lululxvi/deepxde)**<br>PINN and inverse-flow experiments | Scientific machine-learning library supporting physics-informed networks and neural operators. Includes differential-equation workflows useful for fluid inverse problems, parameter estimation and exploratory surrogate studies with explicit boundary and initial conditions.<br>[Docs](https://deepxde.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `LGPL-2.1` | Established · PINN training convergence is problem dependent; verify predictions against trusted numerical or experimental references. |
| **[EZyRB](https://github.com/mathLab/EZyRB)**<br>Nonintrusive parametric CFD surrogates | Data-driven reduced-basis modelling from parameterized numerical snapshots. Useful for constructing nonintrusive CFD surrogates when full-order results are available but modifying the original governing-equation implementation is impractical.<br>[Docs](https://mathlab.github.io/EZyRB/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Prediction quality depends on snapshot coverage; extrapolation beyond the training parameter range needs testing. |
| **[ITHACA-FV](https://github.com/mathLab/ITHACA-FV)**<br>Reduced models built around OpenFOAM | OpenFOAM-based reduced-order-modelling framework with methods for developing compact finite-volume models. Useful for experimenting with projection, parameterized flow snapshots and reduced solvers close to existing OpenFOAM workflows.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/mathLab/ITHACA-FV#readme) · `LGPL-3.0` | Research · Check the supported OpenFOAM release and rebuild dependencies for the selected branch. |
| **[NeuralOperator](https://github.com/neuraloperator/neuraloperator)**<br>Fourier neural-operator baselines | Official neural-operator library containing Fourier Neural Operators and related architectures. Useful for learning mappings between PDE inputs and solution fields, including fluid and transport surrogate research.<br>[Docs](https://neuraloperator.github.io/dev/) · `MIT` | Research · Resolution transfer does not guarantee accuracy under new geometries, regimes or boundary conditions. |
| **[NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo)**<br>Scalable engineering surrogates | PyTorch-based physics machine-learning framework with reusable models and training recipes. Aerodynamics examples make it relevant to surface and volume surrogates, scalable training and engineering field prediction.<br>[Docs](https://docs.nvidia.com/physicsnemo/latest/) · `Apache-2.0` | Established · GPU requirements, model prerequisites and separate dataset terms vary across examples. |
| **[PDEArena](https://github.com/pdearena/pdearena)**<br>Fluid surrogate benchmark experiments | Training and evaluation framework for neural PDE modelling, with architecture collections and fluid datasets. Useful for controlled comparisons of learned Navier–Stokes and shallow-water evolution models across spatial and temporal scales.<br>[Docs](https://pdearena.github.io/pdearena/) · `MIT` | Research · Research benchmarks and trained models do not establish performance on industrial geometries or operating regimes. |
| **[PhiFlow](https://github.com/tum-pbs/PhiFlow)**<br>Differentiable fluid-learning experiments | Differentiable simulation toolkit integrated with NumPy and machine-learning frameworks. Provides fluid examples and automatic differentiation pathways useful for inverse problems, learned corrections and optimization through simulations.<br>[Docs](https://tum-pbs.github.io/PhiFlow/) · `MIT` | Research · Choose the numerical model and discretization for the intended physics; differentiability alone does not establish fidelity. |
| **[PyDMD](https://github.com/PyDMD/PyDMD)**<br>Flow-snapshot modal analysis | Dynamic mode decomposition tools for extracting coherent spatial and temporal structures from snapshot data. Useful for analyzing CFD transients, building data-driven reduced models and comparing modal representations.<br>[Docs](https://pydmd.github.io/PyDMD/) · `MIT` | Established · Mode estimates depend on sampling, noise and preprocessing; the fitted dynamics need independent validation. |
| **[pyMOR](https://github.com/pymor/pymor)**<br>Projection-based reduced models | Model-order-reduction library using abstract interfaces to connect numerical algorithms and PDE backends. Supports reduced bases, POD and system-theoretic approaches for constructing compact parameterized flow or transport models.<br>[Docs](https://docs.pymor.org/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Connecting a new CFD solver requires compatible operators, data access and suitable reduction assumptions. |
| **[PySINDy](https://github.com/dynamicslab/pysindy)**<br>Data-driven fluid dynamics identification | Sparse system-identification tools for discovering compact dynamical models from data. Useful for fluid reduced coordinates and PDE-discovery experiments where interpretable model structure is preferable to a black-box surrogate.<br>[Docs](https://pysindy.readthedocs.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Derivative estimation, noise and candidate-library choices strongly affect discovered equations. |
| **[Transolver](https://github.com/thuml/Transolver)**<br>Geometry-aware aerodynamic surrogates | Research implementation of transformer-based PDE modelling on general geometries. Includes airfoil and car-flow design experiments, making it useful for studying geometry-aware surrogates alongside established neural-operator baselines.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/thuml/Transolver#readme) · `MIT` | Research · Published benchmark claims belong to the original study; reproduce the selected task before relying on generalization. |

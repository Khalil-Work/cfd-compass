## 🌀 Turbulence, DNS, LES & HPC

Research solvers, adaptive grids and large-scale flow computing.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[AFiD](https://github.com/PhysicsofFluids/AFiD)**<br>Canonical convection and rotating-wall DNS | Parallel finite-difference flow solver for Rayleigh-Benard convection and Taylor-Couette turbulence, built around distributed decompositions and FFT-based algorithms for large canonical direct numerical simulations on HPC systems.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/PhysicsofFluids/AFiD#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Specialized canonical-flow geometry and boundary conditions; check variant-specific documentation and dependencies rather than treating it as a general geometry solver. |
| **[CaNS](https://github.com/CaNS-World/CaNS)**<br>Canonical wall-bounded turbulence DNS | Parallel incompressible Navier-Stokes solver designed for canonical-flow DNS, using a structured finite-difference discretization and efficient pressure solution with CPU and GPU-oriented implementations for turbulence research at scale.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/CaNS-World/CaNS#readme) · `MIT` | Research · Targets canonical structured domains; check grid, boundary-condition and accelerator constraints instead of assuming arbitrary industrial geometry support. |
| **[Dedalus](https://github.com/DedalusProject/dedalus)**<br>Custom spectral fluid-dynamics models | Spectral PDE framework with symbolic equation specification and parallel execution, supporting fluid-dynamics research across Cartesian and curved domains rather than restricting users to one fixed equation set.<br>[Docs](https://dedalus-project.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `GPL-3.0` | Established · Users formulate equations, gauge conditions and boundary constraints; this flexibility is not a substitute for checking mathematical well-posedness and numerical convergence. |
| **[FluidSim](https://github.com/fluiddyn/fluidsim)**<br>Reproducible Python turbulence experiments | Python framework for reproducible fluid-dynamics simulations with extensible solvers, MPI-enabled pseudospectral methods, and integrated output and analysis workflows for turbulence and geophysical-flow research across multiple model equations.<br>[Docs](https://fluidsim.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Official publish-only GitHub mirror; canonical development and contributions are on the project's Heptapod Mercurial repository. |
| **[IAMR](https://github.com/AMReX-Fluids/IAMR)**<br>Variable-density incompressible AMR studies | Parallel adaptive-mesh code for variable-density incompressible Navier-Stokes equations, providing an AMReX-based route to locally refined flow simulations and exploration of block-structured algorithms for research applications.<br>[Docs](https://amrex-fluids.github.io/IAMR/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Variable-density incompressibility is a modeling assumption, not a fully compressible shock-capturing formulation; check the examples and required AMReX revision. |
| **[incflo](https://github.com/AMReX-Fluids/incflo)**<br>Adaptive embedded-boundary incompressible CFD | AMReX-based incompressible Navier-Stokes application for flows in complex geometries, combining block-structured adaptive-grid infrastructure with embedded boundaries and shared hydrodynamic utilities for parallel CPU and GPU research.<br>[Docs](https://amrex-fluids.github.io/incflo/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · Requires compatible AMReX and AMReX-Hydro versions; follow project instructions for supported solvers and boundary conditions rather than assuming general model coverage. |
| **[Oceananigans.jl](https://github.com/CliMA/Oceananigans.jl)**<br>GPU geophysical and ocean turbulence | Julia software for incompressible geophysical fluid dynamics on CPU and GPU hardware, supporting Cartesian and spherical-shell domains, turbulence studies and ocean-oriented models with programmable simulation workflows.<br>[Docs](https://clima.github.io/OceananigansDocumentation/stable/) · `MIT` | Established · Ocean-oriented incompressible and hydrostatic/nonhydrostatic modeling assumptions differ from general compressible industrial CFD; choose the documented model appropriate to the scales. Its documented ocean and Boussinesq formulations do not cover arbitrary compressible engineering flows. |
| **[OpenSBLI](https://github.com/opensbli/opensbli)**<br>Generated compressible DNS solvers | Python code-generation framework translating symbolic compressible-flow equations into parallel high-order finite-difference solvers, with structured curvilinear multiblock meshes and WENO or TENO shock-capturing options for heterogeneous computing architectures.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/opensbli/opensbli#readme) · `GPL-3.0` | Research · Generated solvers depend on a compatible parallel runtime and compiler stack; changing symbolic equations still requires numerical verification. |
| **[Pencil Code](https://github.com/pencil-code/pencil-code)**<br>Compressible turbulence and MHD research | Modular high-order finite-difference code for compressible hydrodynamics with magnetic fields and particles, offering MPI-parallel infrastructure and many sample configurations for turbulence and astrophysical-fluid research on distributed systems.<br>[Docs](https://pencil-code.org/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · The project documents its own source-management and contribution workflow; numerical diffusion and shock treatment must be selected for the intended regime. |
| **[spectralDNS](https://github.com/spectralDNS/spectralDNS)**<br>Python spectral DNS research | Python collection of parallel spectral Navier-Stokes and related solvers, providing an inspectable environment for direct numerical simulation and experiments with spectral discretization and time-integration methods.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/spectralDNS/spectralDNS#readme) · `LGPL-3.0` | Research · Research-oriented solver collection; verify the maintained solver path and current shenfun/MPI dependency compatibility for the intended example. |
| **[WaterLily.jl](https://github.com/WaterLily-jl/WaterLily.jl)**<br>Scripted immersed-body flow experiments | Julia solver for unsteady incompressible viscous flow around dynamic immersed bodies, using Cartesian grids, geometric multigrid pressure solves and backend-agnostic CPU or GPU execution in two or three dimensions.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/WaterLily-jl/WaterLily.jl#readme) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · An immersed Cartesian-grid method rather than a body-fitted mesh solver; quantify boundary and grid-resolution effects for the target geometry. |
| **[Xcompact3d / Incompact3d](https://github.com/xcompact3d/Incompact3d)**<br>Structured-grid incompressible DNS and LES | High-order compact finite-difference framework for turbulent-flow DNS and LES, combining Cartesian-grid efficiency, distributed-memory parallelism and immersed-boundary treatments for research on incompressible flows and complex geometries.<br>[Docs](https://xcompact3d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Established · The named repository is the CPU solver; inspect the separate accelerator developments rather than assuming identical GPU capabilities. |

## 🔗 Coupled physics & specialist applications

Fluid-structure interaction, porous flow, biomedical flow and design.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[DAFoam](https://github.com/mdolab/dafoam)**<br>Adjoint aerodynamic and multidisciplinary optimization | Adds discrete-adjoint capabilities to OpenFOAM-based multiphysics analysis with a Python interface for optimization. Supports aerodynamic, thermal and coupled design studies with many design variables.<br>[Docs](https://dafoam.github.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Requires compatible OpenFOAM and optimization dependencies; convergence of the primal and adjoint problems matters for usable design gradients. Use the documented solver and dependency versions; verify adjoint gradients for the chosen objective and case. |
| **[DuMux](https://github.com/dumux/dumux)**<br>Porous-media and multidomain transport | Models porous-medium flow, transport and deformation with finite-volume and control-volume finite-element methods. Includes coupled multidomain formulations for applications that cross free-flow and porous regions.<br>[Docs](https://dumux.org/) · `GPL-3.0` | Established · The GitHub repository is an official mirror; primary development and issue tracking are hosted by the Stuttgart GitLab project. |
| **[IBAMR](https://github.com/IBAMR/IBAMR)**<br>Immersed-boundary fluid-structure interaction | Combines immersed-boundary methods with Cartesian adaptive mesh refinement and distributed-memory parallelism. Useful for deformable structures interacting with fluids while retaining separate Eulerian and Lagrangian descriptions.<br>[Docs](https://ibamr.github.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Requires a substantial numerical-library stack; discretization and coupling choices must match the stiffness, resolution and boundary conditions of the problem. |
| **[MRST](https://github.com/SINTEF-AppliedCompSci/MRST)**<br>Reservoir simulation and carbon storage | Provides a MATLAB toolbox for flow, transport and mechanics in porous media. Includes modular reservoir and carbon-storage workflows that support numerical experimentation and model development.<br>[Docs](https://www.sintef.no/projectweb/mrst/) · `GPL-3.0` | Established · Upstream recommends packaged releases for most users; compatibility with GNU Octave varies by module and should not be assumed. |
| **[oomph-lib](https://github.com/oomph-lib/oomph-lib)**<br>Finite-element multiphysics method development | Offers an object-oriented finite-element library with extensive multiphysics demonstration problems. Useful for building fluid-mechanics and fluid-structure models when researchers need control of the governing formulation.<br>[Docs](https://oomph-lib.github.io/oomph-lib/doc/html/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · A programmable numerical library rather than a turnkey engineering interface; model formulation and solver choices remain the user's responsibility. |
| **[OpenPNM](https://github.com/PMEAL/OpenPNM)**<br>Pore-network transport modeling | Represents porous materials as pore networks and solves transport across them. Useful when investigating effective flow and transport properties without directly resolving every pore-scale fluid volume.<br>[Docs](https://openpnm.org/) · `MIT` | Established · Network representations simplify pore geometry and physics; they are not interchangeable with fully resolved pore-scale Navier-Stokes simulations. |
| **[OPM Flow](https://github.com/OPM/opm-simulators)**<br>Reservoir-scale multiphase simulation | Implements reservoir flow simulation with automatic differentiation and established reservoir input formats. Useful for multiphase subsurface studies that need well models and reservoir-scale fluid transport.<br>[Docs](https://opm-project.org/) · `GPL-3.0` | Established · Reservoir models operate under subsurface-flow assumptions and should not be substituted directly for free-fluid CFD. |
| **[preCICE](https://github.com/precice/precice)**<br>Coupling independent physics solvers | Connects existing simulation programs through partitioned coupling, interface data exchange and mapping. Provides a common route to fluid-structure interaction, conjugate heat transfer and other coupled workflows.<br>[Docs](https://precice.org/docs.html) · `LGPL-3.0` | Established · A coupling library, not a CFD solver. Stable coupling still depends on compatible adapters, interface conditions and convergence settings. |
| **[solids4foam](https://github.com/solids4foam/solids4foam)**<br>Finite-volume fluid-solid interaction | Extends the OpenFOAM ecosystem with solid-mechanics and fluid-solid interaction models. Provides tutorials for comparing partitioned coupling approaches and examining deformation-driven changes to a flow domain.<br>[Docs](https://www.solids4foam.com/) · `GPL-3.0` | Established · Compatibility is OpenFOAM-version dependent; verify the supported fork and version rather than mixing tutorial generations. |
| **[svMultiPhysics](https://github.com/SimVascular/svMultiPhysics)**<br>Cardiovascular multiphysics and fluid-structure research | Provides coupled finite-element models for fluids, solids, diffusion and electrophysiology in cardiovascular research. Supports investigations where blood flow and deforming biological tissue must be modeled together.<br>[Docs](https://simvascular.github.io/documentation/multi_physics.html) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · Formerly svFSIplus; research software does not itself establish clinical suitability. Patient-specific predictions require separate validation and appropriate expertise. |

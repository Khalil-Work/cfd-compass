## 🔥 Combustion, chemistry & reacting flows

Chemical kinetics, flames, reacting-flow solvers and fire simulation.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[ABLATE](https://github.com/UBCHREST/ablate)**<br>Hybrid-rocket combustion and turbulent entrainment | Develops reacting-flow capabilities around turbulent mixing, fuel entrainment and ablative boundary layers. Offers a research entry point for studying combustion environments associated with hybrid rocket motors.<br>[Docs](https://ablate.dev/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · Research-oriented software; use documented examples and confirm the available physical models before planning a new application. |
| **[Cantera](https://github.com/Cantera/cantera)**<br>Ignition, flame-speed and mechanism studies | Provides thermochemistry, transport properties, reaction mechanisms, reactor networks and one-dimensional flames. A practical foundation for checking chemistry assumptions before embedding them in a reacting-flow solver.<br>[Docs](https://cantera.org/stable/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Not a general three-dimensional CFD solver; mechanism suitability must be assessed for the intended conditions. |
| **[Fire Dynamics Simulator](https://github.com/firemodels/fds)**<br>Fire, smoke and building-flow research | Models low-speed fire-driven flows with large-eddy simulation, emphasizing smoke and heat transport. Its documentation includes separate verification and validation material for assessing intended fire-engineering applications.<br>[Docs](https://pages.nist.gov/fds-smv/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · A low-speed fire model rather than a general shock or detonation solver. Safety-critical conclusions require qualified interpretation and appropriate validation. |
| **[PeleC](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PeleC)**<br>Compressible combustion and shock-flame interaction | Solves compressible reacting flows using block-structured adaptive meshes and embedded boundaries. Connects detailed chemistry to flow calculations where acoustic waves and compressibility are physically important.<br>[Docs](https://pele-suite.github.io/PeleC/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Requires the AMReX, PelePhysics and SUNDIALS stack; mesh, chemistry and temporal resolution need problem-specific assessment. |
| **[PeleLMeX](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PeleLMeX)**<br>Low-Mach turbulent combustion | Combines a low-Mach reacting-flow formulation with adaptive refinement and embedded boundaries. Targets flame-turbulence research while avoiding the acoustic time-scale restriction of a fully compressible formulation.<br>[Docs](https://pele-suite.github.io/PeleLMeX/) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Research · The low-Mach formulation is unsuitable when shocks or strong acoustic coupling govern the physics. Use the documented model assumptions. |
| **[PelePhysics](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PelePhysics)**<br>Chemistry, spray and soot infrastructure | Supplies thermodynamics, transport, chemistry generation and additional spray, soot and radiation modules shared by Pele solvers. Useful when researching combustion models rather than duplicating property implementations.<br>[Docs](https://pele-suite.github.io/PelePhysics/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · A supporting physics library rather than a complete standalone CFD application; module support differs between Pele solvers. |
| **[pyJac](https://github.com/SLACKHA/pyJac)**<br>Analytical chemical Jacobian generation | Generates analytical Jacobian source code for chemical reaction mechanisms. Helps researchers examine the expensive derivative calculations that arise inside stiff chemical-kinetics integrators and reacting-flow applications.<br>[Docs](https://slackha.github.io/pyJac/) · `MIT` | Research · An older research tool with version-sensitive dependencies; generated code and mechanism support should be checked in the target environment. |
| **[pyMARS](https://github.com/Niemeyer-Research-Group/pyMARS)**<br>Chemical mechanism reduction | Reduces detailed chemical mechanisms using graph-based methods and sensitivity analysis. Useful for preparing smaller chemistry models when detailed mechanisms make reacting-flow or reactor studies too expensive.<br>[Docs](https://niemeyer-research-group.github.io/pyMARS/) · `MIT` | Research · Reduction accuracy depends on the selected targets and training conditions; validate the reduced mechanism over the intended operating envelope. |
| **[Reaction Mechanism Generator](https://github.com/ReactionMechanismGenerator/RMG-Py)**<br>Building chemical mechanisms | Constructs chemical kinetic mechanisms using reaction families and thermochemical data. Supports upstream chemistry development for combustion studies before mechanism reduction, reactor assessment or coupling to CFD.<br>[Docs](https://reactionmechanismgenerator.github.io/RMG-Py/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Generated mechanisms are hypotheses requiring assessment against suitable data; this is not a spatial CFD solver. |
| **[Spitfire](https://github.com/sandialabs/Spitfire)**<br>Flamelet tables and reaction-diffusion studies | Builds tabulated chemistry models and integrates chemistry or reaction-diffusion systems. Particularly relevant to nonpremixed flamelet libraries used to reduce the cost of larger fire simulations.<br>[Docs](https://spitfire.readthedocs.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Tables remain specific to their generating assumptions, mechanism and parameter range; extrapolation requires separate assessment. |
| **[TChem](https://github.com/sandialabs/TChem)**<br>Chemical kinetics kernels and reactor models | Provides chemical source terms, thermodynamic properties, Jacobians and canonical reactor formulations for complex kinetic mechanisms. Useful for integrating and studying the stiff chemistry component of reacting simulations.<br>[Docs](https://github.com/sandialabs/TChem#readme) · `BSD-2-Clause` | Established · Distinguish this kinetics toolkit from the separate TChem-atm atmospheric-chemistry project; it does not supply a complete spatial flow solver. |

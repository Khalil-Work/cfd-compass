## 🌍 Ocean, atmosphere, wind & environmental flow

Geophysical, coastal, hydrological and wind-energy modelling.

| Project / use case | What it offers | Selection notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[ADCIRC](https://github.com/adcirc/adcirc)**<br>Storm surge and coastal circulation | Models time-dependent free-surface circulation and transport on unstructured finite-element grids. Common application areas include storm surge, tides, wind-driven circulation and coastal flooding studies.<br>[Docs](https://adcirc.github.io/adcirc/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Requires suitable meshes, forcing and calibration for the site; official documentation is being consolidated across several maintained sources. |
| **[ANUGA](https://github.com/anuga-community/anuga_core)**<br>Flood and tsunami inundation | Solves shallow-water equations for floods, tsunamis and storm surges using finite-volume methods. Provides Python workflows and examples for combining terrain, boundary conditions and inundation studies.<br>[Docs](https://anuga.readthedocs.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Depth-averaged shallow-water assumptions do not resolve detailed vertical flow structure; terrain and boundary-data quality strongly affect inundation results. |
| **[CM1](https://github.com/NCAR/CM1)**<br>Idealized convection and storm research | Supports idealized atmospheric experiments with a nonhydrostatic numerical model. Useful for studying convection, clouds and organized storms while controlling the background state and experimental forcing.<br>[Docs](https://cm1.readthedocs.io/) · `MIT` | Established · Primarily an idealized research model; configuring a real-time operational forecast is a different task from running its standard experiments. |
| **[Delft3D](https://github.com/Deltares/Delft3D)**<br>Integrated hydrodynamics and morphodynamics | Collects Delft3D structured and flexible-mesh simulation engines for hydrodynamics, waves, morphology and water quality. Useful for integrated coastal, estuarine, river and urban water studies.<br>[Docs](https://oss.deltares.nl/web/delft3d) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · The source engines and packaged graphical interfaces have different delivery arrangements; component licensing and build requirements should be checked individually. |
| **[ERF](https://github.com/erf-model/ERF)**<br>Adaptive atmospheric and energy-flow studies | Builds atmospheric-flow modeling on AMReX with adaptive meshes and a documented theory and examples. Relevant to energy-related atmospheric research and simulations requiring local resolution refinement.<br>[Docs](https://erf.readthedocs.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Check the supported atmospheric models and boundary treatments for the intended application; the solver remains an evolving research code. |
| **[FUNWAVE-TVD](https://github.com/fengyanshi/FUNWAVE-TVD)**<br>Dispersive coastal-wave transformation | Models dispersive nearshore waves with a Boussinesq-type formulation and shock-capturing treatment. Useful for wave transformation, coastal wave generation and studying processes beyond nondispersive shallow-water models.<br>[Docs](https://fengyanshi.github.io/build/html/index.html) · `NOASSERTION` | Research · Upstream labels the default branch beta and directs users to released versions. Confirm licensing and the release appropriate to your study. |
| **[GeoClaw](https://github.com/clawpack/geoclaw)**<br>Tsunami propagation and inundation | Extends Clawpack for geophysical waves and flows, including tsunami propagation and inundation. Uses adaptive refinement to concentrate resolution where evolving waves and coastal interactions require it.<br>[Docs](https://www.clawpack.org/geoclaw.html) · `BSD-3-Clause` | Established · Depth-averaged flow assumptions and topographic resolution constrain the phenomena represented; assess the model for the intended hazard application. |
| **[Kynema-SGF (formerly AMR-Wind)](https://github.com/kynema/kynema-sgf)**<br>Wind-farm wakes and atmospheric boundary layers | Uses an adaptive structured-grid incompressible formulation for atmospheric boundary layers and wind-farm wakes. Can serve as the background flow solver in coupled blade-resolved wind-energy simulations.<br>[Docs](https://kynema.github.io/kynema-sgf/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Renamed from AMR-Wind in 2026; actuator models and blade-resolved overset configurations represent different levels of physical detail. |
| **[Kynema-UGF (formerly Nalu-Wind)](https://github.com/kynema/kynema-ugf)**<br>Geometry-resolved wind and energy-system flows | Provides an unstructured incompressible flow solver used in the Kynema wind-energy ecosystem. Useful for geometry-resolved turbine and energy-system flow calculations alongside structured background simulations.<br>[Docs](https://kynema.github.io/kynema-ugf/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Renamed from Nalu-Wind in 2026; coupled overset studies require additional ecosystem components and matching configuration. |
| **[MITgcm](https://github.com/MITgcm/MITgcm)**<br>Ocean circulation and geophysical fluid dynamics | Models ocean and atmospheric circulation with a configurable numerical core and extensive verification examples. Useful across geophysical scales, including nonhydrostatic ocean processes and large-scale circulation.<br>[Docs](https://mitgcm.readthedocs.io/) · `MIT` | Established · Configuration, forcing and parameterizations determine the applicable scale; reproducing an experiment requires more than selecting a solver executable. |
| **[MOM6](https://github.com/NOAA-GFDL/MOM6)**<br>Ocean circulation and Earth-system modeling | Solves ocean primitive equations with flexible vertical coordinates and options for coupled Earth-system configurations. Useful for ocean circulation studies and research into ocean-model numerics and parameterizations.<br>[Docs](https://mom6.readthedocs.io/) · `Apache-2.0` | Established · Designed for ocean primitive-equation modeling rather than general free-surface engineering CFD; regional setups need appropriate forcing and boundary data. |
| **[MPAS-Model](https://github.com/MPAS-Dev/MPAS-Model)**<br>Variable-resolution geophysical modeling | Provides a shared unstructured-mesh framework with atmosphere, ocean and other geophysical model cores. Useful for multiscale circulation studies where mesh design and regional refinement matter.<br>[Docs](https://mpas-dev.github.io/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Individual cores have separate build instructions, capabilities and data requirements; the shared repository is not a single universal model. |
| **[Proteus](https://github.com/erdc/proteus)**<br>Hydraulic multiphase and numerical-method research | Separates continuum physical models from numerical methods in a Python-led toolkit. Includes multiphase Navier-Stokes, shallow-water and porous-flow formulations relevant to hydraulic and coastal research.<br>[Docs](https://proteustoolkit.org/) · `MIT` | Established · Broad method availability does not make every formulation suitable for every problem; external compiled dependencies are part of the installation. |
| **[SCHISM](https://github.com/schism-dev/schism)**<br>Cross-scale river-estuary-ocean circulation | Uses unstructured grids for three-dimensional baroclinic circulation across connected river, estuary, shelf and ocean scales. Useful when one study spans several interacting hydrodynamic environments.<br>[Docs](https://schism-dev.github.io/schism/) · `Apache-2.0` | Established · Choose supported modules and document forcing and vertical-grid assumptions; development-branch flags can change between model versions. |
| **[Thetis](https://github.com/thetisproject/thetis)**<br>Coastal circulation and adjoint studies | Builds coastal and estuarine flow models on Firedrake finite elements. Includes shallow-water, tracer, wetting-and-drying and adjoint capabilities for studying circulation and inverse problems.<br>[Docs](https://thetisproject.org/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Development versions may require matching Firedrake development branches; shallow-water assumptions must fit the intended physical scales. |
| **[WRF](https://github.com/wrf-model/WRF)**<br>Weather and mesoscale atmospheric modeling | Provides the Weather Research and Forecasting modeling system for atmospheric research and numerical weather prediction. Useful when CFD studies need meteorological context or mesoscale flow conditions.<br>[Docs](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/users/) · `NOASSERTION` | Established · Meteorological predictions depend on initialization, forcing and physical parameterizations; WRF is not a replacement for geometry-resolved local engineering CFD. |
